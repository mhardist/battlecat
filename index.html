Buying here is different. Here's how.

Hawai'i real estate runs on rules and vocabulary that mainland search sites were never built for. The essentials, in plain language:

Leasehold, explained

On most of the mainland you always buy the land. On O'ahu, a large share of condos sit on land owned by estates and trusts. Buying leasehold means you own the home but rent the ground under it.

  • Lease rent is a monthly payment on top of your mortgage and maintenance fee.
  • Expiration matters most. Under about 35 years left, financing gets hard; under 20, plan on cash.
  • Renegotiation dates can step the rent up sharply mid-lease.
  • Fee available means you can buy the land and convert to fee simple.

True monthly cost

A $299k leasehold in Waikiki can cost more per month than a $455k fee simple in Mo'ili'ili. Every card here totals mortgage, maintenance fee, lease rent, property tax, and insurance, and shows which utilities the maintenance fee already covers. Honolulu's home exemption and Residential A tier are built into the estimate.

CPR detached homes

Many "condos" on O'ahu are actually houses. A Condominium Property Regime splits one lot into separately sellable homes. It looks like a house, titles like a condo, and often shares a driveway.

Short-term rentals

Honolulu's Bill 41 sets a 90-day minimum rental outside resort zones. If a listing is marketed for vacation-rental income, check the badge: only resort-zoned properties qualify legally.

Cesspools

Hawai'i requires every cesspool converted by 2050, typically $20k to $50k. Wastewater type is a real line item here, so it's a filter, not fine print.

Water on all sides

Coastal O'ahu listings carry FEMA flood zones (AE and VE drive mandatory insurance), tsunami evacuation zones, and the state's sea-level-rise exposure disclosure. We surface all three on every listing instead of leaving them to escrow week.

Hawaiian Home Lands

DHHL homesteads are 99-year leases at $1 per year, reserved for beneficiaries of 50% or greater Native Hawaiian ancestry. They're a distinct market with their own badge and eligibility rules, treated with the clarity they deserve.

Browse the island by region

O'ahu shops by coastline and climate: windward green, leeward sun, town convenience, country quiet. Mauka is toward the mountains; makai is toward the sea.
